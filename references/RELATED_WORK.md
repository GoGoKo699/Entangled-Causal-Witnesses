# Related work and attribution

This project asks how the **initial spatial entanglement budget** controls the strength of a reduced temporal witness for one fixed family of joint measurement channels. The figure of merit is $`f(R)=\|R\|_1-1`$, twice the sum of the absolute values of the negative eigenvalues of a Hermitian, trace-one pseudo-density matrix (PDM). A negative PDM eigenvalue is temporal nonpositivity; it is not an entanglement measure of an ordinary density matrix.

For $`A,B\simeq\mathbb C^d`$, the apparatus measures the maximally entangled effect $`E_d=|\Phi_d\rangle\langle\Phi_d|`$ and retains a binary output after a symmetric readout error of probability $`q`$. Both initial subsystems enter the apparatus. With initial Schmidt number at most $`k`$, the [scientific account](../science/README.md) establishes

```math
F_k(d,q)=\Theta_{q_0}\!\left(\frac{k^2}{d(k+qd)}\right),
\qquad 1\le k\le d,\quad 0\le q\le q_0<\frac12.
```

Thus fixed positive readout noise changes the optimized dependence from linear to quadratic in $`k/d`$. The known PDM representation, witness framework and general usefulness of entanglement are foundations of the result. The matching all-input resource law for this channel family is the additional statement.

## PDMs, causal soundness and product witnesses

**Fitzsimons, Jones and Vedral (2015), “Quantum correlations which imply causation.”** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep18281); [arXiv:1302.2731](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.2731). This work introduces the PDM construction and its trace-norm causal measure. We inherit the use of temporal nonpositivity to distinguish correlations that cannot be represented by an ordinary spatial density matrix.

**Liu, Qiu, Dahlsten and Vedral (2025), “Quantum causal inference with extremely light touch.”** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-024-00956-0); [arXiv:2303.10544v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.10544v4). Theorem 1 and Eq. (5) supply the Jordan-product PDM with the input-transposed Choi convention. Theorem 3 proves that a channel nonsignalling from $`B_1`$ to $`A_2`$ has a positive reduced PDM for **every** initial state, including correlated states. This supplies the causal soundness of our entangled preparations. The paper conjectures qualitative detection by a pure product input and separately suggests that pure products might maximize negativity. These are different assertions: existence of a product witness does not imply that it is quantitatively competitive. Our family separates them by exhibiting constant entangled-input negativity while the best separable-input value decays quadratically at fixed noise. We do not claim the Jordan formula or the semicausal null theorem as new.

**Authored trureturing proof dossier, “Every finite-dimensional signalling channel has a pure product input with positive PDM negativity.”** [Dossier and linked formal source](https://www.trureturing.org/research/liu-qiu-dahlsten-vedral-2025-pure-product-signalling-negativity/), accessed 13 November 2026. It records a formal settlement of the qualitative conjecture for arbitrary finite dimensions. Its stated theorem uses the input-transposed Choi convention and a Jordan-block nonpositivity argument. We inspected the stated scope and argument, but did not independently compile its Lean source. We treat the qualitative existence statement as prior work; this project does not claim to settle it. The dossier does not assert product-input maximization or a dimension-independent lower bound on witness strength.

## Temporal witness framework

**Liu, Verma, Xiao, Dahlsten and Gu (2025), “Spatial Incompatibility Witnesses for Quantum Temporal Correlations.”** [arXiv:2511.01179v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.01179v2), revised 13 November 2025. Definition 1 relates spatial incompatibility to distance from density matrices. Definition 2 and Proposition 1 use positive semidefinite operators to witness temporal nonpositivity, and explain their implementation through sequential correlators. The paper also gives channel-dimension bounds and shows how initial coherence and measurement disturbance can reveal temporal nonpositivity even for classical channels. We inherit this witness language and the negative-subspace construction. Our two-context estimator specializes that framework to an attaining preparation; it does not introduce the general idea of a temporal witness. The additional resource statement concerns the initial **bipartite Schmidt number**, the uniform readout-noise crossover, and the formation entanglement required for constant negativity in the fixed apparatus.

## Schmidt number and channel discrimination

**Terhal and Horodecki (2000), “Schmidt number for density matrices.”** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.61.040301); [arXiv:quant-ph/9911117v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9911117v4). The convex-roof Schmidt-number definition is inherited. Lemma 1 gives the familiar maximally entangled overlap bound $`\operatorname{Tr}(E_d\rho)\le k/d`$. That overlap bound alone gives only a linear upper estimate here. The stronger quadratic estimate at fixed noise uses a completed-square bound on the negative parts of the reduced output blocks, followed by convexity over all Schmidt-number-bounded mixed preparations.

**Bae, Chruściński and Piani (2019), “More Entanglement Implies Higher Performance in Channel Discrimination Tasks.”** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.140404); [arXiv:1809.02082v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.02082v1). Section II.A, Eq. (13), characterizes Schmidt number through advantage in a suitable binary channel-discrimination task. Section II.B identifies Schmidt-number robustness with optimized multiplicative multichannel advantage. This establishes the general usefulness of higher Schmidt number. Its channel acts on a probe while the ancillary subsystem is untouched, and the objective is a guessing probability optimized over tailored discrimination tasks. Here both $`A`$ and $`B`$ enter one specified joint-effect apparatus, and the objective is reduced-PDM trace negativity. Their theorem does not directly give this apparatus's quadratic noise law, its matching crossover or its two-context temporal estimator.

## Formation entanglement and weak-value amplification

**Vedral and Plenio (1998), “Entanglement measures and purification procedures.”** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.57.1619); [arXiv:quant-ph/9707035v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9707035v2). We use relative-entropy entanglement and its lower bound on entanglement of formation, established in their Theorem 6. Combining these standard tools with data processing for the Bell-effect measurement and this project's negativity bound shows that constant witness strength requires $`\Omega_q(\log d)`$ formation entanglement at fixed positive noise. This is an entanglement-of-formation statement; it is not an identification of the exact one-shot Bell-pair preparation cost for arbitrary mixed inputs.

**Pang, Dressel and Brun (2014), “Entanglement-Assisted Weak Value Amplification,”** [PRL 113, 030401](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.113.030401), and **Pang and Brun (2015), “Suppressing technical noise in weak measurements by entanglement,”** [PRA 92, 012120](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.92.012120). These works already show that entanglement can improve postselection efficiency and protect weak measurements against readout errors. The latter analyzes readout-induced inaccuracies, Fisher information and majority-vote postselection. Consequently, the broad message that entanglement can improve noise robustness is not new. Our estimator retains both apparatus outputs, subtracts statistics from two measurement contexts, and has an all-Schmidt-number converse for temporal negativity. We claim no metrological Fisher-information improvement or universal sample-complexity advantage from that negativity gap.

## Broader spatiotemporal-state formulations

**Jia and Xiao (2026), “Unified spatiotemporal quantum states and spatiotemporal entanglement from Kirkwood–Dirac phase space.”** [arXiv:2609.24680v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24680v1). The work unifies several temporal-operator constructions. Its Section 7.1 and Proposition 7.3 analyze separability-based robustness of a temporal operator, and Section 6 treats vectorized temporal Schmidt spectra. Those resources concern the resulting spatiotemporal operator rather than the initial spatial Schmidt-number constraint used here. The comparison covers these relevant definitions and propositions, rather than a complete audit of the paper.

## Scope of the additional contribution

The new account is a sharp resource hierarchy for an engineered family, with the same channel used for every allowed preparation at each $`d,q`$. The upper bound covers classical shared randomness and mixed preparations; the lower construction and two-context witness retain the full initial-entanglement and joint-apparatus costs. Generic Bell-overlap suppression, qualitative product detection, the existence of temporal witnesses and the general benefits of entangled probes remain credited to prior work. No arbitrary-channel optimality, exact universal optimal constant, or experimental sample-complexity separation is inferred. The comparisons above are targeted primary-source comparisons, not an exhaustive priority claim.
